Runze Zhang

I’m a Milnor Lecturer at Stony Brook University. I earned my PhD from Université Paul-Sabatier in Toulouse, 2024, under the supervision of Pascale Roesch.

My research focuses on holomorphic dynamics of dimension one. I'm particularly interested in neutral dynamics as well as renormalization techniques.

I'm currently on jobmarket.
